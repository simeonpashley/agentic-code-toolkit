# Launch Checklists

## Waitlist

- domain works
- SSL works
- form works
- confirmation works
- support/contact available
- privacy notice for email collection
- analytics/source tracking
- spam protection if needed
- post-signup question
- backup/export of leads

## Beta

- onboarding works
- core task works
- support channel
- feedback channel
- known limitations listed
- error monitoring
- admin/user lookup
- rollback/feature flag
- invite tracking
- privacy/terms updated

## Public Launch

- all critical paths tested
- billing if paid
- cancellation if paid
- incident process
- status page or comms route
- accessibility baseline
- security basics
- regulatory review
- support coverage
